## 1. Chapter Vision

- **Working Title**: Chapter 2: The Western Ghats Sky Temple
- **Environment**: High-altitude cloud temple complex perched on sheer basalt cliffs of the Western Ghats mountain range.
- **Atmospheric Palette**: Sweeping mist, morning horizon blues, wet basalt stone, Tibetan/Deccan alpine wind flags, golden sunlight breaking through clouds.

---

## 2. Planned Core Mechanics

1. **Aerodynamic Platforming & Wind Currents**:
   - Directional mountain wind gusts that influence jump velocity and trajectory.
   - Wind-sheltered alcoves where precision timing is required to cross open ravines.
2. **Solar Mirror Alignment Puzzles**:
   - Rotating brass celestial sundials to redirect reflected sunlight across mountain towers to melt ancient frozen mechanisms.
3. **Climbing & Ledge Traversal**:
   - Extending Rudhra's verbs with vertical wall ledges and rope ascents.

---

## 3. Narrative Progression

- Having unlocked the first cosmic key (the *Surya-Chakra*) in the Sunken Sanctum, Rudhra tracks the celestial coordinates toward the western peaks.
- Clues reveal that three planetary relics must be gathered before the rare solar eclipse alignment to prevent cosmic atmospheric disruption on Earth.
