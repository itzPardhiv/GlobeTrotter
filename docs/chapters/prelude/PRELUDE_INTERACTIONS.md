# GlobeTrotter: The Lost Relic — Prelude Interactions & Dialogue Scripts

## 1. Sequence 3: Bounty Confrontation Dialogue Script

The dialogue sequence between Rudhra and Vikram establishes character personalities, tone, and the premise of the one billion gold coins:

- **Line 1 [VIKRAM]**: "You followed me all the way out here for a bounty? You have no idea what you've walked into."
- **Line 2 [RUDHRA]**: "Funny. Most people start talking when they realize they have nowhere left to run."
- **Line 3 [VIKRAM]**: "I'm serious. There are things buried out there that should never be disturbed."
- **Line 4 [RUDHRA]**: "Then you won't mind telling me why you were carrying a map."
- **Line 5 [ACTION]**: *Vikram hesitates. Rudhra notices the old map on the ground.*
- **Line 6 [RUDHRA]**: "Now that's interesting."
- **Line 7 [VIKRAM]**: "Leave it alone."
- **Line 8 [RUDHRA]**: "What's it lead to?"
- **Line 9 [VIKRAM]**: "One billion gold coins. That's what the stories say."
- **Line 10 [RUDHRA]**: "One billion?"
- **Line 11 [VIKRAM]**: "Coordinates are marked on the map. An ancient temple. No one who's gone looking has come back with the treasure."
- **Line 12 [RUDHRA]**: "And you expect me to believe you just happened to have it?"
- **Line 13 [VIKRAM]**: "I went looking. I found something else. Something I wish I'd never seen."
- **Line 14 [ACTION]**: *Rudhra examines the map carefully.*
- **Line 15 [RUDHRA]**: "Well, you've just made my day a lot more interesting."
- **Line 16 [VIKRAM]**: "Listen to me. If you reach that temple, don't touch anything you don't understand."
- **Line 17 [RUDHRA]**: "That's generally how I get paid."
