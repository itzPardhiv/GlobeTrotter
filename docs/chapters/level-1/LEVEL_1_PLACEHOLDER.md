# GlobeTrotter: The Lost Relic — Level 1: The Forgotten Sanctum (Specification & Implementation Status)

> [!NOTE]
> **Implementation Status**: FULLY IMPLEMENTED & INTEGRATED as Sequences 6 through 12 of the playable adventure.

## 1. Chapter Overview

- **Title**: Chapter 1: The Sunken Sanctum
- **Environment**: Vast subterranean rock-cut temple complex inspired by Ellora Caves and Konark Sun Temple.
- **Atmosphere**: Deep cavern darkness, ancient banyan root intrusions, crumbling sandstone lintels, and shafts of celestial daylight piercing high apertures.
- **Primary Mechanic**: Dynamic brass lantern illumination reveals hidden bas-relief murals and Sanskrit inscriptions.
